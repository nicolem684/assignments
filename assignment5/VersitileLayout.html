Entertainment in D.C.

As the capitol of the United States, Washington D.C. has a large cultural scene and entertainment industry! While D.C. is known for its history and monuments, the city has even more to offer.

We have marked some of our favorite places around the district, including concert venues, bars, theaters, and bike racks. These local spots are must-sees on your next trip to D.C. There is so much to explore!
