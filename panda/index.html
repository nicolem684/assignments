Panda Diplomacy

How the spatial distribution of pandas in captivity showcases China's diplomatic relationships. Updated!

The current panda diplomacy program stipulates that every panda, including those born outside of the country, legally belongs to China and are loaned out to other countries instead of transferring ownership. In the 1970’s and 1980’s, the program was much simpler as China would gift pandas to other countries with no strings attached.
